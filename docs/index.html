detectadeal CLI: Amazon India and Flipkart price drops, price history and coupon codes from the terminal

An open-source price tracker for Indian online shopping. One command shows today's biggest price drops across Amazon India, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Croma, Nykaa, JioMart and 250+ stores. Every result links to its price history on detectadeal.com, so you can tell a real discount from a marked-up list price.

Install

Needs Node.js 18 or newer.

npm install -g detectadeal
detectadeal --help

Commands

CommandWhat it does
detectadeal dropsToday's biggest price drops. Same data as Price Drops on detectadeal.
detectadeal search tws earbuds -p 1500Search deals across stores, with a max price.
detectadeal deals -c mobiles -d 30Browse a category with a minimum discount. See mobile deals.
detectadeal coupons myntra --codes-onlyActive coupon codes. Full list at detectadeal offers.
detectadeal categoriesAll deal categories with live counts.
detectadeal watch iphone 15 -b 55000Poll a search and print deals at or below your price. For push alerts use detectadeal price alerts.
--jsonMachine-readable output on every command.

Why price history matters

Indian sale events, from Big Billion Days and the Great Indian Festival to Diwali and Prime Day, are full of discounts measured against inflated list prices. A price history chart shows the real lowest price of the last months, so you know whether to buy now or wait. The CLI gives you the list; the detectadeal deal pages give you the chart, the lowest-ever price, similar deals and a one-click price alert.

About detectadeal

detectadeal.com is a free deal finder and price tracker for Indian online shopping. It tracks prices across Amazon India, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa, Croma, JioMart, Meesho, Tata CLiQ and hundreds more, and gives every product a price history, a real-discount check and free price-drop alerts. There is a Telegram channel and an Android app.
