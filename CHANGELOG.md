# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-16)

First release.

- `drops`: today's biggest price drops across Amazon India, Flipkart, Myntra and 250+ stores.
- `deals`: browse live deals by category, minimum discount, store and sort order.
- `search`: deal search with store and max-price filters.
- `coupons`: active coupon codes and store offers, with a codes-only mode.
- `categories`: deal categories with live counts.
- `watch`: poll a search and print deals that reach your price.
- `--json` on every command for scripts.
- Every result links to its price history page on detectadeal.com.
